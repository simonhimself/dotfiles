description: Explain what a company actually does in plain language
---
I need to understand what $ARGUMENTS actually does as a business. Cut through
any marketing language, buzzwords, and jargon.

Tell me:
1. What the company does in one sentence a non-technical person would understand
2. Who their customers are and what problem they solve for them
3. How they make money (business model)
4. What their core product/technology actually is
5. Who they compete with

If I've pasted in content from a deck or website, base your analysis on that.
If I've just given a company name, research what you can and be clear about
what you're confident about vs. what you're inferring.

Be direct. No filler. If something is genuinely unclear even after analysis,
say so rather than guessing.
